Load your Memory Stick

Shelf Tools reads what Sidequest, Checkpoint, Emblem, Runtime and Shelf keep in their folders. Files are read here in your browser and stay on your computer or phone.

  1. On the PSP, open Shelf's PS-button menu and choose USB connection (or use the XMB's USB mode).
  2. Pick the Memory Stick's drive below. Only the five plugin folders are read; ISOs and saves are skipped.
Drop the Memory Stick, its plugin folders, or a zip of them here

If the browser will not open the drive itself, select the five plugin folders in your file manager and drag them onto this box together.

On a phone, a folder cannot always be picked: zip SIDEQUEST, CHECKPOINT, EMBLEM, RUNTIME and SHELF on a computer and pick the zip. Save-state files are large; leaving SIDEQUEST out only drops the slot pictures.
