Sync

Keeps your saves, achievements, journey and time played level between your PSPs and PS Vitas. Each one is synced with a vault, a folder on this computer, and the vault passes on what the others added. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing is written until you press Sync now.

1 Memory Stick

Put the PSP in USB mode (Shelf's PS-button menu, USB connection) and pick its drive. On a PS Vita, pick the pspemu folder on the card.

2 Vault

A folder of its own on this computer, the same one every time. For the first sync, make an empty folder, such as Documents/Shelf Vault, and pick it.

Everything is read in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Only Sync writes to a Memory Stick, and only when you press Sync now. For Shelf and psp-ark-plugins · Shelf Tools

Load your Memory Stick

Shelf Tools reads what Sidequest, Checkpoint, Emblem, Runtime and Shelf keep in their folders. Files are read here in your browser and stay on your computer or phone.

  1. On the PSP, open Shelf's PS-button menu and choose USB connection (or use the XMB's USB mode).
  2. Pick the Memory Stick's drive below. Only the five plugin folders are read; ISOs and saves are skipped.

On a PS Vita (Adrenaline 8): the Memory Stick is the pspemu folder on your card, usually ux0:pspemu. Pick that folder, not the card itself. If the computer asks to format the card, do not: the wrong USB device is chosen in Adrenaline's menu.

Drop the Memory Stick, its plugin folders, or a zip of them here

If the browser will not open the drive itself, select the five plugin folders in your file manager and drag them onto this box together.

On a phone, a folder cannot always be picked: zip SIDEQUEST, CHECKPOINT, EMBLEM, RUNTIME and SHELF on a computer and pick the zip. Save-state files are large; leaving SIDEQUEST out only drops the slot pictures.
